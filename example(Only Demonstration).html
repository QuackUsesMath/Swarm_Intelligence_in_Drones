    How the swarm works

    No central controller. Each drone keeps its own private map, sharing it by gossip with neighbours in radio range. It bids on frontier cells (cost = distance, battery, crowding, old trails), plans with budget-limited A*, and yields by ID priority to avoid collisions. Missing heartbeats mark a peer failed and free its claimed targets. Modelled after the OptiForge repo README.
